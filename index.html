About the studio

ARDS is founded on the belief that good architecture comes from clarity: a clear brief, a clear design and a clear process from concept to completion. Our aim is to deliver architecture, interior and project management services that are functional, carefully detailed and delivered as promised.

Residential

The Ivory Residence

Islamabad | 15,485 sft | 2026

The Ivory Residence is a two-storey neo-classical home built around a double-height arched entrance and a central fountain. Its symmetrical facade brings together fluted pilasters, balustraded balconies and slate-grey mansard roofs, softened by a formal landscape of clipped hedges and conifers. Architecture, interiors and project management were handled together so the design stays consistent from the facade to the finishes.

Our role: Architecture, Interior and Project Management
